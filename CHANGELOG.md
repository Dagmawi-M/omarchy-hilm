# Changelog

## v1.4.0
- **Transitions** (`v`): Omarchy's default, or fade, wipe, grow, outer, wave, center, left, right, top, bottom or random on Omarchy 3 (via awww). They play while browsing, when applying and when cancelling. The choice is saved.

## v1.3.0
- **Undo** (`u`, or `omarchy-hilm undo`): restores the previous wallpaper and theme, up to 20 steps back.
- **Online search** (`w`): searches wallhaven.cc (SFW only). Enter downloads the image into your collection and applies it.

## v1.2.0
- Every Omarchy theme's backgrounds are included alongside your own collection.
- Source chips: All · My collection · Current theme · All themes (Tab / click).
- Having your own collection is now optional in the installer.

## v1.1.x
- Omarchy 4 support: Lua keybindings, `omarchy-shell` backgrounds, and the new colour names.
- Always runs with the system Python, so mise/pyenv/conda can't hide GTK.
- Symlinked wallpapers are counted and rotated correctly.
- README screenshot. Unicode symbols switched to Nerd Font icons.

## v1.0.0
- First release: cover-flow picker with live preview, automatic re-theming, favourites, search, random spin, auto-rotate, import, non-destructive hide, and the installer wizard.
